# Specialist-agent map

**Event router**

→ Revenue and cashiering  
→ Bank and card reconciliation  
→ Cash integrity  
→ Restricted funds  
→ Interfund  
→ Payroll and position funding  
→ Grants and SEFA  
→ Reporting and disclosures

Each specialist workflow publishes evidence and results into the common **evidence-lineage layer**.

Evidence lineage → required-role verification and reviewer routing → controlled posting service → independent verification.

Agents have bounded responsibilities. They can identify exceptions, assemble evidence, and propose treatment. They cannot manufacture authority, approve their own work, or bypass controlled posting and verification gates.
