Municipal Finance Control Layer

Modernizing municipal finance without replacing the systems already in place.

MFCL integrates existing accounting/ERP, utility billing/cashiering, banking, payroll, grants/projects, and supporting documents into one controlled municipal finance layer.

Existing-system positioning: Designed to integrate with existing and legacy QuickBooks Desktop environments; functionally tested with QuickBooks Desktop 2022 in a controlled test environment. No replacement ERP is required.

AI interprets context.

Documents and operational context are organized into review-ready explanations.

Controls decide.

Structured checks determine whether an item clears, stops, or requires a qualified role.

People authorize.

Municipal officials and qualified reviewers retain factual verification, judgment, and approval authority.

One layer across the municipal finance lifecycle

ERP integration

Connect existing accounting systems while preserving them as systems of record.

Revenue and cashiering

Link receipts, ownership, settlement, and accounting without relying on collection location.

Bank and card evidence

Use independent cash evidence for reconciliation and exception review.

Restricted funds

Test purpose and authority before an item can move to approval.

Payroll and grants

Connect position funding, award terms, eligibility, and expenditure evidence.

Close and reporting

Preserve lineage through statements, notes, SEFA, and audit support.

Public boundary: Every person, transaction, amount, fund example, and document shown here is fictional. This interface contains no client data, credentials, proprietary matching logic, posting implementation, or production accounting rules.
